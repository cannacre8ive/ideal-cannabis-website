## Guardrails

- Do not invent or silently update potency, terpene, batch, availability, retailer, licensing, or contact claims.
- Do not add commerce or ordering flows without age, jurisdiction, compliance, fulfillment, and payment review.
- Preserve the original supplied prototype under `source/` whenever production files change.
- Keep the static architecture until an approved integration creates a real server-side requirement.
